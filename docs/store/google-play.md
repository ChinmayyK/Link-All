## Short description (80 max)

## Full description (4000 max)
Link All brings continuity to every operating system. Your Android phone works hand in hand with your Windows PC, Mac and Linux machine, in any mix, the way devices from a single company usually only do with each other.

Copy on your phone and paste on your computer. Share a photo or a whole folder to your PC from any app. When your phone rings, your computer shows who is calling, and your notifications appear on your desktop. From the phone, browse the folders your computers share and fetch the file you need.

Everything travels straight over your Wi-Fi, LAN or hotspot, encrypted end to end. No cloud, no account, no tracking. Nothing you copy or send ever leaves the devices you paired.

ONE CLIPBOARD
• Copy text, links or images on any device and paste on any other.
• A searchable history of what you shared, with pinning.
• Passwords, one-time codes, card numbers and API keys stay on the device they were copied on.

FILES AND FOLDERS
• Send files or whole folders to your computers, and receive theirs.
• Share straight from any app's share menu, with your connected computers listed first.
• Transfers show live progress and resume after a dropped connection.

YOUR PHONE ON YOUR COMPUTER
• Notifications appear on your computer as they arrive. Leave out any app you like.
• Incoming calls show who is calling.
• Your computer shows the phone's battery and free space.

YOUR COMPUTERS ON YOUR PHONE
• Browse, search and preview the files a computer shares, then download just the one you need. Each computer decides whether to allow it.

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
• Pair once by confirming the same PIN on both screens, or by scanning a QR code.
• Everything is encrypted end to end, directly between your devices.
• Light and dark themes, and a home screen widget.
• Free and open source: github.com/ChinmayyK/Link-All

Link All for Windows is on the Microsoft Store. Get it for Mac and Linux at linkall.chinmaykudalkar.com.

## Data safety form (answers for the play build)
- Data collected: none. Data shared: none. Link All has no server; data moves only between the user's own paired devices on their network.
- Encrypted in transit: yes.
- Users can request deletion: not applicable (nothing is collected); uninstalling removes local data.
- Notification access: used to show the phone's notifications and the caller's name on the user's paired computers. Not collected or sent anywhere else.

## Claims to keep out (the play build doesn't do these)
- Answering or declining calls from the computer.
- SMS one-time code forwarding.
- A computer browsing the phone's files.
These need the Android build from the website; mention them only as "more on the website", never as Play features.
